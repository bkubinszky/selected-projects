Agentic Commerce

Machine-Readable Product Catalogue for AI Agents & Applications

Date
2026
Status
WT
Working Theory: a preliminary assumption that serves as a starting point for further investigation.

Problem statement & Goal

Most product data is primarily designed for human users and conventional online shops. AI applications, LLMs and autonomous agents, however, require structured and semantically enriched information to reliably find, compare and recommend products.
Objective

“Development of an application that makes existing legacy product catalogues machine-readable by transforming, enriching and providing product information in a structure optimised for LLMs and AI agents.”

Approach & Workflow

This project is currently in the ideation phase. I am researching similar existing solutions and conducting small tests to assess the feasibility of individual components.

Working Theory: Development of a prototype product catalogue for selected outdoor brands. Product data is aggregated from different sources, normalised and enriched with additional metadata. The information is then stored in a structured JSON architecture that can be processed directly by LLMs, RAG systems or agents.

Key Learnings

01
Ideation Phase

This project is currently in the ideation phase, with new insights being added continuously.

02
Data Quality

Structured data is a fundamental requirement for reliable AI applications. The quality of product recommendations depends heavily on the semantic modelling of the underlying data.

03
SEO + AEO

Traditional e-commerce data is often not optimised for AI use cases. Until now, the main focus has been on SEO to reach potential customers. As agentic commerce becomes more common in e-commerce, this is no longer sufficient and needs to be complemented by additional AEO measures.
